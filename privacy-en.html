Swing Sword Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how the operator of "Swing Sword" (스윙 소드, the "App") handles your information. The operator processes personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and, for users in the EU and UK, the GDPR and UK GDPR.

1. Information we process

The App has no sign-up or login. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location (neither GPS location nor location estimated from your IP address).

While you use the App, the following information is generated and sent automatically:

  • Usage records: app launches and exits, swings, monsters defeated, enhance attempts and results, new swords obtained, play time and other in-game activity
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, whether a motion sensor is available, and your device's language and region settings and time zone (used to produce per-country usage statistics)
  • A random identifier the App creates for each device (this is not an advertising identifier)

Your game progress (gold, sword level and so on) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the operator.

2. Why we process it

  • To understand, through statistics, how much and how players use the App, so we can improve the game

We do not use this information for advertising, marketing or tracking you across other apps or websites, and we do not try to identify individual users from it.

3. Processors and international transfer

To produce these statistics we use the processor below, and your information is transferred outside Korea in the process.

ItemDetails
RecipientPostHog, Inc. (analytics service)
CountryUnited States
Information transferredUsage records, device information, and random identifier described in section 1
When and howContinuously while you use the App, over encrypted connections (HTTPS)
PurposeUsage statistics for the App
Retention12 months from collection

We do not provide your information to any other third party.

If you do not want your information transferred, stop using the App and delete it; no further information will be sent. The App has no separate in-app switch to turn sending off.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Information is deleted from the analytics service when the retention period in section 3 ends.
  • If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so without delay.
  • Deleting the App also deletes your game progress stored on the device.

5. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing, by emailing the address in section 8. We will act without delay and tell you the result. Because the information is stored only under a random identifier, we may ask for your device model and roughly when you used the App so we can find your records.

6. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Access to the analytics service is limited to the operator, with two-factor authentication on the account
  • We do not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or contact details

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we will delete it without delay.

9. Users in the European Union and the United Kingdom (GDPR and UK GDPR)

  • Legal basis: the operator's legitimate interest in improving the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We collect only the information listed in section 1, none of which directly identifies you.
  • International transfer: information is transferred to the United States (PostHog, Inc.) under the safeguards PostHog provides, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
  • Your rights: you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to processing and data portability, and you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country. Send requests to the email in section 8.
  • We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page at least 7 days before the change takes effect.
